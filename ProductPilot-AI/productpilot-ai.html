How ProductPilot AI works. Every check on this dashboard is calculated on this page with built-in rules, so no connection is needed and nothing is sent anywhere. Your data stays in this browser until you export it.

Requirements

Paste the requirements your workflow produced, one per line. ProductPilot AI uses the product idea from the Overview.

Workflow output

Lines starting with "As a" become user stories. Lines starting with "Given", "Test" or "TC" become test cases.

Feedback input

One item per line. Start with High, Medium or Low to set its priority. Items without one show as Unrated.

Industry headlines

Should you build this idea?

Compares your product idea with the headlines on the left and gives a verdict. Headlines count only when they share key words with your idea.

Change Impact Analyzer

Describe a change you are thinking about. You see which requirements, stories and tests it touches, how big the change is and what to do about it.

Requirement to Test Coverage

Each requirement is matched to the stories and test cases that share its key words. A requirement with a test is Covered, with only a story is Weak, with neither is a Gap. Matching is by words, so review it with your team.
