Product idea

A student food delivery app designed for college students to order affordable meals and snacks from nearby restaurants and campus food outlets. Students can browse menus, compare prices, place orders, make payments, and track their deliveries. The app focuses on affordable food, quick delivery to college campuses and hostels, and a simple, convenient ordering experience for students.

  1. R01Students can create an account and log in securely.
  2. R02Students can browse nearby restaurants and campus food outlets.
  3. R03Students can view food menus, prices, and descriptions.
  4. R04Students can search for food items and filter them by category.
  5. R05Students can add food items to their cart and update quantities.
  6. R06Students can place food orders and select a delivery location.
  7. R07Students can make payments using available payment methods.
  8. R08Students can track their order status in real time, where supported.
  9. R09Students can receive notifications about order confirmation and delivery updates.
  10. R10Students can view their previous orders and reorder meals.
  11. R11The app displays affordable meal options suitable for student budgets.
  12. R12Restaurant partners can manage menus and update food availability.
  13. R13Delivery partners can view assigned orders and update delivery status.

Test cases6

  • Given a student opens the restaurant list, When nearby restaurants are available, Then the app displays restaurant names and menus.
  • Given a student adds a food item to the cart, When the cart is opened, Then the selected item, quantity, and price are displayed correctly.
  • Given a student places a valid order, When the order is confirmed, Then the app displays an order confirmation.
  • Given a student opens order tracking, When the order status changes, Then the app displays the latest available status.
  • Given a restaurant marks an item unavailable, When students view the updated menu, Then the item is shown as unavailable.
  • Given a student enters invalid login details, When they attempt to log in, Then the app displays an appropriate error message.
